---
name: yt-condense
description: What `ko yt -s` asks Gemini when it watches a video — edit to change every write-up
---
Write up this video for someone who won't watch it but needs everything in it.

Keep every point of substance, in the order it comes, with its detail: what was said, shown or done; numbers with their units; prices; comparisons; what each person liked and disliked and exactly why; what they felt or noticed while using something; where something went wrong, what exactly happened and what they did about it; caveats, corrections and changes of mind. Say who holds each opinion. A point made once in passing still counts.

Include what is shown on screen but not said aloud (tables, prices, readouts, text), marked "(on screen)".

Cut only what carries no information: greetings, sponsor reads, requests to like and subscribe, repetition and banter. Never cut a specific to save space, and never turn a specific into a generality ("they had some issues" instead of what the issues were). Don't add facts or judge.

Length follows the content: as long as it takes to keep every point, no longer. Use the video's chapters as headings if it has them, otherwise short topic headings, each with the timestamp where it starts. Plain markdown, terse sentences or bullets. Start with the first heading: no preamble, no closing summary.
